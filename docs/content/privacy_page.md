# Privacy

Career Coach is a university project built by a team of students. It is not a registered company. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to your information.

## What we collect, and why

- **Your account:** your email and password, so you can sign in.
- **Your profile:** name, city, university, degree, major, CGPA, graduation year, skills, languages and experience. We use these to suggest career tracks.
- **Your quiz answers:** to work out which career tracks suit you.
- **Your CV, if you upload one:** your browser reads the text in your PDF. The PDF file itself is not uploaded. We save the text as your base CV and send it to our AI to fill in your profile form.
- **Later features:** if you use features such as the vent space, we will store what you write so the feature can work.

## What is optional

You can skip all of these:

- Your LinkedIn link
- Gender
- Nationality
- Year of birth

Gender and nationality are used **only** to check eligibility rules, for example a scholarship open only to women or only to Pakistani nationals. We never use them to choose career suggestions.

## LinkedIn import

We read your public LinkedIn profile **only when you ask us to**, by pressing the import button. Nothing is saved to your profile until you have checked the details and confirmed them.

## Who can see your data

- **You** can see all of your own data.
- Other students cannot see your data.
- The team's admin can see usage counts, for example how many times a feature was used. The admin cannot read your vent-space messages.

## How we use AI

Some features send the relevant details you have given us to an AI service, which writes suggestions. We do not send your gender, nationality or year of birth when choosing career tracks.

**AI can be wrong.** Please check any suggestion before you rely on it. Career Coach is not a counsellor or a financial adviser. If you are struggling, please talk to someone you trust.

## Deleting your data

To delete your account and data, email careercoachproject27@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and tell us you want it deleted.

## Questions

Career Coach is a university project. If you have a question about your privacy, contact careercoachproject27@gmail.com.
